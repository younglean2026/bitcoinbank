The mechanism

Trading fees in. Bitcoin out.

A simple loop that never stops: volume on $RBTC funds WBTC buys, and WBTC lands in holder wallets on Ethereum every five minutes.

STEP 1

$RBTC trades on Robinhood Chain

Every buy and sell of $RBTC generates a trading fee. 100% of fees are routed to the bank treasury.

→
STEP 2

The treasury buys WBTC

Collected fees are used to market-buy WBTC — wrapped Bitcoin — on Ethereum. The bank holds nothing back.

→
STEP 3

Holders get paid every 5 minutes

WBTC is distributed pro-rata to every wallet holding at least 30,000 $RBTC — directly on Ethereum, no claim needed.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I start earning WBTC?

Buy and hold at least 30,000 $RBTC in your wallet on Robinhood Chain. That's it — your wallet is automatically included in every distribution cycle.

Where do the rewards come from?

Every $RBTC trade pays a fee. 100% of those fees are used to buy WBTC on Ethereum, which is then split pro-rata among eligible holders.

Why do rewards arrive on Ethereum and not Robinhood Chain?

WBTC's deepest liquidity lives on Ethereum. Paying rewards there means you receive an asset you can immediately hold, sell, or use across DeFi.

Do I need to claim my rewards?

No. Distributions are pushed directly to the Ethereum address associated with your holding wallet, every 5 minutes, 288 times a day.

What happens if my balance drops below 30,000 $RBTC?

Your wallet is paused from distributions until the balance is back above the threshold. Top back up and you're automatically re-enrolled in the next cycle.
